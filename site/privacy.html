▦ Local Sketch Deck

PRIVACY

Nothing is watching your deck.

Local Sketch Deck stores your project only in the file you save. It has no analytics, advertising, account system, cloud sync, or telemetry.

If you purchase a supporter license, Sociobot/Dodo handles checkout as merchant of record. The landing page stores the license token and its cached verification result in your browser’s local storage so it can restore your unlock. License verification contacts Sociobot only when online, at most once per day.

Contact: privacy@sociobot.in.
